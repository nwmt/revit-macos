# Revit 2025

**✅ Works** on 2025.5 (build 25.5.0.57). Tested by hand on real work in a test project.

## Works

- **Sign-in and files:** sign-in through your Mac's browser, opening a project, saving. Revit closes normally.
- **Modeling and views:** walls, views, 3D, text, Materials, families; the start screen, Manage Links and web panels.
  The RFO Benchmark runs to the end.
- **Output:** export to PDF, DWG, DXF, DGN, DWF and IFC; printing to **Revit PDF** (files go to
  `~/Documents/Revit-PDF`).
- **Dynamo and add-ins:** Dynamo 3.3 (graphs, node library, preview in Revit views) and Steel Connections; pyRevit
  7.0.0 and RevitLookup 2025.2.4 load and their windows open. `./revit addins 2025` also trusts their publishers, so
  Revit does not ask **Always Load** on every start.

## Known issues

- **The first print to Revit PDF sometimes produces nothing.** Print again.
- **The status bar at the bottom of the window is sometimes blank** where it should show progress, for example
  during IFC export.
- **"Do not show again" is not remembered** in Revit's other dialogs: the same question comes back on the next start.
- **Generative Design is turned off.** It fails to load with an error on start.
